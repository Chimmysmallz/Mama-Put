# Mama Put: privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Mama Put: Buka Rush is a free browser game. We built it to collect as little as possible.

## What the game stores
- Your game progress (days, stars, money in the game, upgrades, decorations, helpers and settings) is saved **in your browser on your device only** (local storage).
- The game uses **no cookies, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking**.

## What leaves your device
Nothing, unless you turn on an online feature in Settings. Before you can, the game asks you to confirm you are 18 or older, or that a parent or guardian agrees.

- **Online leaderboard (optional):** your chosen nickname and your Rush Hour score (with the date and which buka you play in) are sent to the game's leaderboard database (Supabase) and shown publicly to other players. Please use a nickname, not your real full name.
- **Google save (optional):** if you sign in with Google, your progress file is stored in a hidden app folder in **your own Google Drive**. The game can only see that one file, not your other files. Google's own privacy policy applies to signing in.

## Your choices
- **Turn off online features:** Settings > Privacy > Turn off online features. This clears your nickname and Google sign-in on this device.
- **Delete your progress:** Settings > Reset progress deletes everything stored on this device.
- **Remove a leaderboard entry:** contact the game owner (details below) with your nickname and roughly when you played.

## Children
Players under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before turning on online features.
